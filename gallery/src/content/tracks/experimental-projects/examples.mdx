# Experiments to take apart

## Little world

- **Try:** [Open the pond](#/demo/ecosystem), pause, plant a food trail and press play.
- **Change:** Adjust sensing distance, then change a movement rule in `demos/ecosystem/world.ts`.
- **Observe:** Do creatures find the same food with a shorter sensing distance? Change one rule at a time.

`world.ts` owns rules; `index.tsx` draws SVG and controls. Start with one creature and meal, or remix the example. Try avoidance, regrowing food or differing behaviours.

Three tests cover movement towards food, one-time consumption and pond boundaries. This toy omits reproduction, starvation and persistence.

## Generative postcard

- **Try:** [Open the postcard](#/demo/art), change the dot count, remix and save an SVG.
- **Change:** Adjust the point distribution in `demos/art/art.ts` or colours in `index.tsx`.
- **Observe:** Keep the seed fixed. Which parts repeat? Open the exported SVG independently.

`demos/art/art.ts` generates repeatable seeded points; `index.tsx` draws SVG, adds controls and exports it. This reference is separate from your [creative starter](#/track/experimental-projects).

## Build towards it, or branch

1. Draw one shape in your starter.
2. Generate a few shapes from a rule you choose.
3. Add one control that changes the result.
4. Optionally add reproducibility or saving.

Try spirals, titles, data-driven sizes or a tiny game. Borrow the generator to focus on interaction.
