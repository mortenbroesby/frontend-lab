# Experiments to take apart

## Little world

- **Try:** [Open the pond](#/demo/ecosystem), pause, plant a food trail and press play.
- **Change:** Adjust sensing distance using the controls, then try changing a movement rule in `demos/ecosystem/world.ts`.
- **Observe:** Do creatures find the same food with a shorter sensing distance? Change one rule at a time.

`demos/ecosystem/world.ts` contains the rules; `index.tsx` draws the SVG and supplies controls. Start your own version with one creature and one meal, or change a rule in the completed example. Try avoidance, food that regrows or different behaviours for different creatures.

The three logic tests check movement towards food, one-time consumption and staying inside the pond. This is a toy habitat, not a biological simulation: there is no reproduction, starvation or persistence.

## Generative postcard

- **Try:** [Open the postcard](#/demo/art), change the dot count, remix and save an SVG.
- **Change:** Adjust the point distribution in `demos/art/art.ts` or colours in `index.tsx`.
- **Observe:** Keep the seed fixed. Which parts repeat? Open the exported SVG independently.

**How it works:** `demos/art/art.ts` produces repeatable points from a seed. `demos/art/index.tsx` draws them with SVG, adds controls and serialises the picture for download. This is a completed reference, separate from your [creative starter](#/track/experimental-projects).

## Build towards it, or branch

1. Draw one shape in your starter.
2. Generate a few shapes from a rule you choose.
3. Add one control that changes the result.
4. If useful, add reproducibility or saving; neither is necessary for a first discovery.

Try a spiral, a title, data-driven sizes or a tiny game. Borrow the generator when you want to focus on interaction instead of rebuilding every piece.
