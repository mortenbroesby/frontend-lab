## Draw with rules

Explore a grid, imaginary coral, lettering or a composition of your own. Choose what gives the image its character: repetition, spacing, palette or empty space.

Try <DemoLink id="generative-postcard">Generative postcard</DemoLink>. Keep a seed while changing a parameter, then change the seed. Which features come from the rule, and which from chance?

### Control the randomness

A seeded generator repeats the same sequence for the same seed and algorithm. Reordering its calls changes where those values end up. Calling `Math.random()` during rendering makes a composition harder to reproduce.

Choose what should vary and what should stay stable. Recreating an image needs its rules and parameters as well as its seed. This randomness is for artwork, not secrets.

### Explore your rule

In `demos/generative-postcard/`, `art.ts` generates the shapes; `index.tsx` connects them to SVG, controls and export. Follow one value to the mark it affects, then adapt the demo or [create an experiment](#/create).

Use the [SVG tutorial](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/SVG/Tutorial) for shapes, coordinates and transforms. Compare a rule change with the seed fixed, then try several seeds. Does it produce a family of interesting images or one lucky result?

If exporting matters, inspect the demo's use of [XMLSerializer](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/XMLSerializer). Open the file outside the app: it needs its own styling, dimensions and assets. Release temporary object URLs after use.

Your outcome could be an image, an interactive drawing or a collection. Export is optional.
