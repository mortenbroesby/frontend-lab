<section className="track-method" aria-labelledby="method-heading">
<h2 id="method-heading">How to work on it</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Pick the part you want to try.</strong><p>Describe one action and what happens next. A puzzle, a miniature city or a playable explanation can start with one control too.</p></li>
<li><strong>Make that part work.</strong><p>Use a dot, a button or one sound. Follow a guide or change an existing demo before adding more scenes, rules or controls.</p></li>
<li><strong>Play with it and adjust.</strong><p>Change the timing, the rule or the input. Keep what makes it more interesting, and make it easy to pause, restart or try again.</p></li>
</ol>
</section>
