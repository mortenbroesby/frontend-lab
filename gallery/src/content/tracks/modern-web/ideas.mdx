<section className="track-method" aria-labelledby="method-heading">
<h2 id="method-heading">How to work on it</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Choose one visible behavior.</strong><p>Describe what someone does and what should happen: “Click here, see a message there.” Your own idea is welcome.</p></li>
<li><strong>Get the smallest version working.</strong><p>Follow a guide or adapt a demo. Use a button, a few values or one message before adding more controls and data.</p></li>
<li><strong>Change something and observe.</strong><p>Try a larger input, reload a tab or disconnect a peer. Check what still works and choose the next improvement from what you see.</p></li>
</ol>
</section>
