## Browser experiments

Try, change and observe an example. Rebuild it in [your own demo](#/create), remix locally or follow another idea.

### Device Rally

- **Try:** <DemoLink id="device-rally">Open Device Rally</DemoLink> in two browsers, join the same room and send emoji cheers.
- **Change:** Change a cheer button and receive-side emoji allowlist in `demos/device-rally/index.tsx`. Both devices need the same version.
- **Observe:** Leave and rejoin. Does the peer count change? Do disconnected cheers arrive later?

Discovery needs internet; the page needs localhost or trusted HTTPS. Restrictive networks may block WebRTC. Physical phones and the workshop network remain untested. [Connection setup](#/track/modern-web/devices).

Stretch: share a colour or quiz answer. Trystero handles discovery; you define messages.

### Dependency ripple

- **Try:** <DemoLink id="dependency-map">Select a package</DemoLink>, then compare the consumers of `react`, `react-dom` and `d3-force`.
- **Change:** Change layout or visuals in `demos/dependency-map/index.tsx`; find consumer traversal in `graph.ts`.
- **Observe:** Switch layouts: positions change, relationships do not. Which consumers are direct or indirect?

Arrows go from consumer to dependency; potential impact travels backwards. This snapshot shows declared workspace dependencies, not source imports or proven breakage. External packages are leaves. Rebuild hosted output to refresh it.

Stretch: show the shortest path to an affected workspace. Read [D3 force simulations](https://d3js.org/d3-force/simulation).

### Generative postcard

- **Try:** <DemoLink id="generative-postcard">Open the postcard</DemoLink>, change the dot count, remix and save an SVG.
- **Change:** Adjust the point distribution in `demos/generative-postcard/art.ts` or colours in `index.tsx`.
- **Observe:** Keep the seed fixed while changing the count. What stays repeatable? Open the saved SVG separately.

Stretch: use a spiral or data-driven sizes. Compare [SVG](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/SVG/Tutorial) with Canvas for your idea.

### Keep the interface listening

- **Try:** <DemoLink id="web-worker-comparison">Run the calculation</DemoLink> in a Worker and use the interaction pad. Compare the main thread, starting with a small workload.
- **Change:** Edit `demos/web-worker-comparison/compute.ts`, shared by both modes. Keep workloads bounded.
- **Observe:** Compare responsiveness at the same workload. Times exclude Worker startup and messaging.

Off-thread work does not guarantee faster execution. Stretch: add progress messages or image processing. Read [Using Web Workers](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Web_Workers_API/Using_web_workers).

### Let two tabs talk

- **Try:** <DemoLink id="cross-tab-messaging">Open Signals</DemoLink>, open its second tab and send a message.
- **Change:** Edit the channel name in `demos/cross-tab-messaging/index.tsx`; reload both tabs.
- **Observe:** Only the other tab receives messages. Reopening loses history. Channel names and origins must match.

BroadcastChannel connects local tabs, not separate computers. Stretch: share a palette or drawing event. Read [Broadcast Channel API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Broadcast_Channel_API).
