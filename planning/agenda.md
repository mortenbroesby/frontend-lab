## Facilitation notes

- Track pitches should help people choose by curiosity, not prior expertise.
- Suggest pairs or small groups across squads; exact group sizes remain open.
- Keep help requests on the board and allow track changes without a heavy process.
- For five presenting groups, suggest five minutes per demo plus brief questions. With more groups, use a gallery or shorter slots within the same 40-minute window; decide once attendance is known.
- Ask each group to show its question, evidence, one surprise and one next step. A failed experiment is welcome.
- Use a screenshot or recorded trace if a live demo is unreliable. Leave findings accessible after the day.
