# Birthday House direction

- Overall: less text, more immersion. Let artwork, lighting, movement and object interactions tell the story. Keep essential clues and help available without persistent explanatory paragraphs.
- Use polished 2D room art with subtle layered animation. Keep rooms illustrated rather than replacing them with modeled furniture. Preserve the cozy, mysterious Danish atmosphere and the moonlit village view.
- Prefer clean, carefully rendered imagery: no added grain, noisy overlays, malformed props or excessive clutter. Inspect artwork and interactive-object placement in portrait and landscape.
- Give the game a beautiful, separate entrance screen with a clear Start Game action. Do not expose gameplay controls before entry.
- Keep the easier puzzles, progress retention, Danish flags and supplied Kasper portrait reveal with blurred background people and Danish birthday decorations.
- Motion stays on with no Stillness toggle, as requested. Keep animation subtle, preserve keyboard access, readable touch targets and concise accessible labels.
- Check actual fullscreen capability. Page-filling layout is not browser fullscreen. For iPhones, provide accurate Home Screen guidance when element fullscreen is unavailable; do not promise unsupported behavior.

- Provide Danish and English for all player-facing text, including clues, hints, accessible labels, display help and the final greeting. Danish is the default; the player chooses a language at the entrance. Keep the phrasing natural, warm and concise. Use “Hint” rather than “vink”.

- When a door opens or unlocks, visibly show it open in the room artwork or rendered scene; a message or clickable hotspot alone is not enough.
- Bring the darkroom and attic artwork up to the main room’s clean, polished standard in both portrait and landscape. Replace malformed props, grain and other AI-generated artifacts while preserving puzzle clarity and the cozy, mysterious atmosphere.

- Preserve the evolving original soundscape, gesture-based start, mute control and suspension while hidden. Sound enriches the experience but never gates progress.
- Keep the living village, optional mischievous nisse, constellation development and paced cinematic photo reveal. Replay must reset both visual and musical progress.

- Room changes must fade out smoothly, wait for the next scene to be ready, then reveal it without flashing artwork, loading overlays or camera jumps. Preserve this alongside all existing requirements.
- Include one mandatory nisse lantern/mirror puzzle and one optional cup trick. Optional rewards must never gate the main journey. Keep both playable without sound or time pressure.
- The final Kasper portrait should sit tightly inside a classic gold painting frame with no pale side bars, against a rich atmospheric backdrop. Preserve the complete photo and a reachable sound control.
