# Birthday melody

The portrait finale uses **I dag er det Oles fødselsdag**, the Danish birthday melody by Otto Mikkelsen, first published in 1913. The game supplies its own quiet instrumental arrangement through Web Audio; it contains no lyrics, sampled recording or copied modern accompaniment.

Sources used to identify the composition and establish its age:

- Jens Stig Olsen, _Skal vi synge den originale eller folkelige version af vores danske sange?_, Dansk Sang 67(1), 2015, pp. 10–17. The [article hosted by Danish University Colleges](https://www.ucviden.dk/ws/files/124410189/Olsen_2015_Skal_vi_synge_den_originale_eller_folkelige.pdf) discusses and reproduces Mikkelsen's 1913 and 1937 versions.
- The [Royal Danish Library's composer catalogue](https://img.kb.dk/export/sites/kb_dk/da/nb/dcm/udgivelser/download/idekatalog.pdf) lists Otto Mikkelsen's life dates as 1868–1942.
- [Danish Copyright Act §63](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2023/1093) specifies the term as 70 years after the author's death year. Together with the composer's dates, this supports treating the underlying composition as public domain in Denmark since 2013. Modern performances and arrangements have separate rights and are not used here.

The optional nisse reward motif is original to this game. All synthesis, voicing, timing, reverb and background accompaniment are generated locally. The melody follows the 1913 scan on article p.13 (PDF page 3), including its E-natural first cadence and original dotted rhythms, played once in F major at 72 quarter-note beats per minute (about 20 seconds). The score streams through a bounded lookahead scheduler; mute, replay and disposal clear pending notes, and opening the portrait again replaces the previous rendition.
